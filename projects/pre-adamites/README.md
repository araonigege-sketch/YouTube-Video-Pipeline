# Who Were the Humans Before Adam and Eve? (pilot for the comparative-religion channel)

First test of the channel's "Case File" look: the cold open (0:00–1:00), built with Cinecode's
conventions (one canvas page, every frame a pure function of time, captured by `capture.mjs`).

- `script.txt`: the owner's full script. `vo/coldopen.txt`: the part rendered so far.
- `film/film.html`: the whole cold open (shot list in its `SHOTS` array, timed to `vo/words.json`).
  `film/map.js` is generated by `tools/mapgen.mjs` (Natural Earth via `world-atlas`).
- `tools/sfx.py`: synthesized foley (paper, stamps, pen, typing, whooshes) → `audio/sfx.wav`.
- `LICENSES.csv` / `credits.md`: every image with its source and licence. `music.json`: the music record.

Media is not committed (`assets/`, `research/`, `audio/`, `film/img/`, `vo/*.mp3`). To rebuild:
fetch the images listed in `LICENSES.csv` into `film/img/` under the names used in `film.html`,
regenerate the narration (ElevenLabs voice "George", `JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb`, with timestamps), then

```bash
CHROME_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1243/chrome-linux64/chrome node ../../.claude/skills/cinecode/scripts/capture.mjs --url film/film.html --video build/picture.mp4 --crf 17 --workers 3 --flags "--no-sandbox"
```

and mix `vo/coldopen.mp3` + `audio/music_bed.wav` (−20 dB, ducked) + `audio/sfx.wav`, loudnorm −14 LUFS.

## The look (liked by the owner, 2026-11-10; voice to be replaced)
- Real archive objects (title pages, engravings, medals, photos) laid on a dark walnut desk, slight rotation,
  paper shadows. No source credits or exhibit numbers on screen; a typed caption (Courier Prime) only when it adds information.
- Vermilion rubber stamps for verdicts (BURNED, ARRESTED, RECANTED, CONDEMNED); a brass loupe to read details.
- Maps and charts on bone paper: engraved coastlines with water-lining, Latin sea names (IM Fell English),
  dashed vermilion routes, "Anno" year card; family-tree and timeline charts (IBM Plex Sans Condensed labels).
- Palette: desk #17120e, paper #e9e0cb, ink #2a221b, vermilion #b3321d. A new visual every ~3–5 s.

## Full episode (v2, 2026-11-10): how it was built
1. **Voice:** Roger (vidIQ voiceover, `CwhRBWXzGAHq8TQ4Fs17`), the Finance pipeline's setup: the script in ≤1,000-character
   blocks (`vo/blocks.json`), then `tools/vo_pace.py --keep` (pauses ≤0.42 s, edges 0.08 s, tempo ×1.05, 0.55 s gaps, 0.5 s lead-in).
2. **Word timings:** `pipeline/align/align.py` per paced block (fallback `align_chunks.py`), with `vo/lexicon.json`
   (ASCII spellings, ARPABET without stress digits) → `vo/words.json`.
3. **Images:** Cinecode's `asset_search.py` (Met, AIC, Cleveland, Rijksmuseum, Openverse, Pixabay) + Internet Archive scans;
   `tools/images.json` maps short keys to sources (`film2/img/<key>.jpg`, ≤2200 px). Every file is in `LICENSES.csv`.
4. **Shots:** `tools/build_shots.py` (the shot list, anchored to narration phrases) → `film2/shots.js`. Long stills are split into
   wide + detail; long charts get camera re-framings, so the frame changes every ~3–5 s.
5. **Picture:** `film2/player.html` (Case File player; maps from `film/map.js` and `tools/mapgen_world.mjs` → `film2/world.js`),
   rendered with Cinecode's `capture.mjs` in two halves (`--start/--end`, JPEG frames), then joined.
6. **Audio:** `tools/mix.py` (narration + 9 generated cues by section + foley from the shot list) → −14 LUFS. See `music.json`.

## v3 (2026-11-10): owner feedback on v2 fixed
- **Framing engine** (`film2/player.html`): stills are `frame` shots in a 16:9 window: `cover` crops clamped inside the image,
  or `fit` (whole image over a blurred copy) for portraits; documents are `page` shots (always whole); `map` shots fit
  the camera to the pins and areas in play and always fill the frame; `footage` plays graded Pexels frames.
- **Shot list**: `tools/shotlist_v3.py` (executed by `tools/build_shots.py`). The builder splits long stills into close-ups
  that respect the source resolution (faces / upper third for figure art; `detail=[(u,v)...]` to steer), and reports long
  holds, late card content, elements that never show, and any image/clip used more than twice (except `INTEGRAL`).
- **Footage**: `tools/footage.json` (Pexels clips, credits), `tools/footage.py --src <dir with footage/>` → `film2/vid/`.
- **QA**: `node tools/qa.mjs film2/player.html` renders every shot at five moments and lists anything outside the
  safe area (5% margin) or any empty frame; must report 0 issues. `node tools/still.mjs film2/player.html <dir> <t...>`
  renders single frames for review.
- **Credits**: `credits.md` is generated from the images and clips actually on screen (for the video description).
